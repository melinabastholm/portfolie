Decoria

A creative project focused on developing interactive posters that combine interior design with personalisation. Our concept allows users to customise a floral poster by changing the vase and flowers, creating an affordable and flexible way to decorate their homes.

Cozy Reads

Cozy Reads is a personal book-tracking website designed to help readers organise their books and keep track of their reading habits. With a cosy and inviting design, users can manage their personal book collection and monitor their reading progress over time.

See more!

Copenhagen Unfolded

Copenhagen Unfolded is a website design concept showcasing the city's attractions, food, and culture. The design combines a consistent visual identity with clear navigation and responsive layouts to create an engaging experience for visitors.
